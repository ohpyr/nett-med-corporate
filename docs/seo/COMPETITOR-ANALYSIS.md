# Competitor Analysis — Montreal Cleaning

## The field

| Competitor | Scale | Model | Threat |
|---|---|---|---|
| themontrealcleaners.ca | 15 yrs, 40+ service pages, bilingual, franchise model | Programmatic service × location | **Highest.** Owns the long tail |
| nettoyageprocleaning.com | 578 Google reviews @ 4.8 | Short, tight, conversion-first pages | **High.** Ranks #1 on the head term |
| menagenrj.ca / menagepromo.ca / menagegopro.com | Mid-size local | Standard service pages | Medium |
| GDI Integrated Facility Services | National, Montreal HQ, publicly traded | Enterprise facility management | Not a competitor — different buyer |
| Scandinavian Building Services | 8,000 staff, 160M sq ft | National retail contracts | Not a competitor — different buyer |

GDI and Scandinavian matter as evidence of what the top of this market looks
like, not as rivals. They bid on national portfolios. Nett-Med competes with
the mid-market local firms in rows 1–3.

## What the winners do that Nett-Med does not

**One page per service, on a keyword URL.** The Montreal Cleaners runs 40+
pages at URLs like `/en/office-cleaning-west-island-montreal/`. Nett-Med
currently has one page total, with three services described in paragraphs on
it. A page cannot rank for a term it does not target, and one page cannot
target forty terms. This is the single largest gap.

**Short pages.** The #1 result for the head term is ~450 words with this exact
structure:

```
H1   Entretien ménager commercial à Montréal
H2   Nos services de nettoyage commercial        → bulleted, each item linked
     Trust bar: 4,8 sur 5 · 578 avis Google · assurés
H2   Contrat régulier ou intervention ponctuelle
H2   Ce qui détermine le prix
H2   Questions fréquentes                        → 3 questions
CTA  Demander ma soumission + phone button
```

No origin story. No mission statement. No stock photo of a smiling person
holding a spray bottle. It answers the four questions a buyer actually has —
what do you clean, how often, what does it cost, are you insured — and then
asks for the job.

**Insurance stated as a headline fact.** "Bonded and fully insured" appears
repeatedly and prominently on every serious competitor. It is the entry
ticket for commercial work.

**Review volume as the primary trust asset.** 578 reviews at 4.8 is a moat
that cannot be copied quickly. Nett-Med has five. This is a real disadvantage
and the site should route around it rather than pretend otherwise — lead with
insurance, tenure, and named testimonials, and run a review campaign in
parallel.

**Explicit contract framing.** Competitors sell the *contract*, not the
cleaning: fixed schedule, single invoice, dedicated contact, 15–20% off annual
terms. That is what a property manager is buying.

## Where the opening is

**Garage washing.** Thin competition. Nett-Med already does it. The buyer is
identical to the common-areas buyer, and the work is annual and contractual.
This is the highest ratio of winnability to contract value on the list.

**English-language commercial terms.** Most Montreal cleaning sites are
French-first with token English pages, or English-first with weak French. A
genuinely equal bilingual build with proper hreflang and native slugs in both
languages is a real structural edge — and downtown/West Island commercial
buyers frequently search in English.

**The syndicat angle.** Common-area maintenance is a legal obligation of the
condominium syndicate. No competitor found leads with this. Content written
directly to that obligation — what the syndicate is required to maintain, how
a contract satisfies it, what deferred maintenance does to valuation — speaks
to the buyer's actual motive rather than to generic cleanliness.

**Specificity over adjectives.** Every competitor writes "professional,
reliable, meticulous." A page that instead says *what is cleaned, at what
frequency, with which product, on whose schedule* will read as more
competent to a buyer and gives search engines far more to match against.

## What not to copy

**Do not clone the 40-page location matrix.** It works for a 15-year-old
domain and will get a new site filtered for thin content. Earn the service
pages first.

**Do not copy the franchise/recruitment content.** The Montreal Cleaners
devotes real estate to selling franchises. That is a different business model
and it dilutes the page for buyers.

**Do not adopt the eco/green positioning as the lead.** It is crowded, it is
unverifiable without certification, and it is not what a syndicat is buying.
Mention Ecologo-certified products as a supporting fact if true — do not build
the brand on it.
